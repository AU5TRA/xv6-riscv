========================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS FILE
========================================================================
This is a plain-English translation of trace.txt, xv6's paging demo
output. Everything that isn't a raw trace event line (banners, phase
headers, the program's own PASS/FAIL narration) is passed through
unchanged. Every "#N TYPE vpn=... slot=... frame=... victim_vpn=..."
line has been rewritten into a sentence.

A few things worth knowing up front:
  - "page" numbers (vpn) are virtual page numbers: the process's address
    space cut into 4096-byte pages and numbered from 0 upward. Low
    numbers here (0-14) are this small demo program's own code/data/
    stack, loaded before the test even starts.
  - "frame" numbers are physical RAM frame indices -- which slot of
    actual RAM a page's data lives in while resident. These are
    reused constantly as pages are evicted and reloaded.
  - "swap slot" numbers are which of the 1024 4KB slots on disk holds a
    page's data while it isn't in RAM.
  - The fields mean different things for different event types (e.g.
    for VICTIM_SELECTED, "vpn" is the NEW page causing the eviction and
    "victim_vpn" is the page being kicked out; for EVICT_END, confusingly,
    "victim_vpn" actually holds the NEW page that will reuse the freed
    frame, not a second victim) -- each translated sentence below spells
    out exactly what's happening rather than making you infer it.
  - A "TRACE_DROP" line isn't a real paging event: it means the fixed-size
    128-event trace ring filled up and the oldest recorded event was
    thrown away to make room. It's expected on the busier phases of this
    demo and does not indicate anything went wrong with paging itself
    (the demo's own PASS/FAIL checks, based on counters rather than the
    trace log, are what actually prove correctness).
========================================================================
